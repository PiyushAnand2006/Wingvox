### A note on AI assistance

The artwork was drafted with AI coding assistance under the project owner's direction.
Under current U.S. Copyright Office guidance, purely machine-generated material is not
protectable; the protectable basis here is the **human** contributions — choosing the
concept, directing the iterations, and selecting/arranging the final composition and
palette. If you register the work (U.S. Form VA, optional but the strongest protection),
disclose the AI-generated material per the Office's current rules; the human-authorship
elements above are what you'd be claiming. This paragraph is practical guidance, not legal advice.

## 5. Care of the mark (keeps it distinctive, which protects the claim)

- Clear space around the mark ≥ the diameter of the voice dot.
- Minimum sizes: vector — free; raster — use the pre-rendered 16/32 px files rather than scaling.
- Don't recolor outside the two provided palettes, rotate, outline, or add effects.
- Don't rebuild the mark from other shapes — always reference these files.
